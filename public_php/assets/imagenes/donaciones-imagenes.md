# Imágenes de ejemplo de Mis donaciones

Escenas y personas ficticias generadas con la herramienta integrada `image_gen`.
Se exportaron a WebP de 960 × 640 para las tarjetas de la vista.

## donaciones-mesa-compartir.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape campaign card photograph for an Argentine solidarity platform, 3:2 framing. Primary request: invented, moving but dignified scene for 'Una mesa para compartir'. In a modest welcoming neighborhood community kitchen in Argentina, two adult volunteers and an older woman share a warm smile while setting a communal wooden table with fresh bread, vegetables and steaming bowls of homemade food. Candid documentary-style fictional photograph, natural skin texture, soft warm window light, genuine connection and hope, everyday clothing. Keep faces and main gesture near center so a 16:10 crop works. No text, logos, watermarks, no distress or pity. Entirely fictional people.

## donaciones-mochilas-futuro.webp

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: landscape campaign card photograph for an Argentine solidarity platform, 3:2 framing. Primary request: invented, moving but dignified scene for 'Mochilas llenas de futuro'. At a welcoming neighborhood community center in Argentina, an adult volunteer crouches to hand a colorful backpack filled with notebooks and pencils to a smiling school-age child accompanied by their mother. Warm candid moment of shared excitement and opportunity, respectful and hopeful, natural documentary-style fictional photography, soft morning daylight, everyday clothing, realistic skin and hands. Keep faces, backpack and gesture centered for a 16:10 crop. No lettering, logos, watermarks, no distress or pity. Entirely fictional people.
